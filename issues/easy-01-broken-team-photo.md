## Description
On `about.html` the barista picture shows a broken-image icon. The image also has no alternative text.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open `about.html`.
2. Scroll to "Meet the barista".

## Expected behaviour
The illustration renders, and screen readers announce a meaningful description.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Image loads correctly
- [ ] `alt` text describes the image (not just "image")

## Files
`about.html`, `assets/`
